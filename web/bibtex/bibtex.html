### About this Parser The **PetitParser BibTeX grammar** parses large bibliography databases in the browser: * **Streaming Parser**: Handles `@article`, `@book`, `@inproceedings`, `@phdthesis`, string macros (`@string`), and comments. * **Resilient Syntax**: Robustly parses nested braces, escaped characters, LaTeX accents, and varied key-value syntax.
