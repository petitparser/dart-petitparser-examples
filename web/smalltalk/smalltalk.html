### Historical Context PetitParser originated in the **Smalltalk** ecosystem (Helvetia) at the University of Bern by Lukas Renggli. * **Message Passing Hierarchy**: Models unary, binary, and keyword message sends with cascading syntax (`receiver msg1; msg2`). * **Blocks & Closures**: Directly models lexical block closures (`[ :arg | ... ]`), temporaries (`| temp |`), and return statements (`^ value`).
