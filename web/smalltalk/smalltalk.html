## About Smalltalk-80 Grammar Smalltalk-80 is an object-oriented, dynamically typed reflective programming language renowned for its elegant, minimal syntax. ### Historical Context - **The Very First PetitParser Grammar**: PetitParser originated in the Smalltalk ecosystem as part of Lukas Renggli's PhD research on **Helvetia** (a dynamic language workbench enabling language embedding into Smalltalk) at the University of Bern. This Smalltalk grammar was one of the very first parsers ever implemented with PetitParser! - **Pure Message-Passing Syntax**: Smalltalk syntax is entirely structured around messages. The grammar models precedence strictly across unary (`receiver msg`), binary (`a + b`), and keyword (`receiver msg: arg1 with: arg2`) messages, as well as cascading (`receiver m1; m2`). - **Rich Typed AST**: The parser builds a complete, strongly-typed AST representing methods, sequences, blocks (`[:arg | ...]`), assignments, return statements, and pragma definitions. - **Interactive Production Switching**: The grammar allows parsing at different root productions: full method definitions, isolated expressions, or statement sequences.
